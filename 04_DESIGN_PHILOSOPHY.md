# 04 — Design Philosophy

## Core idea
**A quiet cockpit for everyday life.** The home screen is the calm track before lights out; useful controls are available immediately, but they never compete with the wallpaper.

## Visual principles
1. Natural first: landscapes, skies, textures and personal wallpapers remain the hero.
2. Racing second: racing influence is expressed through precision, typography, restrained accents and microcopy.
3. Material You foundation: use Android dynamic color when available; do not force a fixed team palette.
4. Information hierarchy: one primary item per screen; supporting content is secondary.
5. Motion with purpose: every animation communicates navigation, state or feedback.
6. Cheeky, not childish: concise copy, occasional wit, no constant jokes.

## Racing-inspired visual vocabulary
- Carbon: dark neutral surfaces and subtle texture.
- Apex: a restrained accent used for primary actions.
- Telemetry: compact secondary information and clear data hierarchy.
- Pit wall: productivity dashboard.
- Grid: app drawer or app collection.
- Formation: space switching or grouped content.
- Checkered motif: rare completion/achievement detail, not a background pattern.

## Optional accent presets
These are original palettes, not copies of official team liveries:
- **Apex Orange:** energetic orange accent with neutral surfaces.
- **Midnight Blue:** deep blue accent for a restrained technical feel.
- **Trackside Green:** muted green accent.
- **Carbon:** monochrome neutral.
Dynamic Material You remains the default when supported. Presets are optional user choices.

## Typography
- Use a highly legible sans-serif for UI.
- Clock may use a distinctive condensed or tabular numeral style.
- Keep body copy readable; do not use racing display fonts for long text.
- Support font scaling and system accessibility settings.

## Iconography
- Prefer simple consistent line icons.
- Do not use official F1, FIA, constructor, sponsor or driver marks.
- Avoid copying another launcher’s proprietary icon or wheel design.
- Icons must remain recognizable at small sizes.

## Microcopy examples
| Context | Copy |
|---|---|
| App drawer | “Lights out. Pick your app.” |
| Search | “What’s the move?” |
| Focus | “No radio. Just focus.” |
| Empty productivity page | “Nothing on the pit wall yet.” |
| Empty widget page | “The grid is empty. Add a widget.” |
| Loading | “Building up the pace…” |
| Low battery shortcut | “Box, box. Find a charger.” |
| Update complete | “Back on the grid.” |

Use microcopy sparingly. Never use jokes in permission explanations, errors involving data loss, privacy disclosures, or accessibility labels.

## Max and Alonso fan references
Use indirect, optional easter eggs only:
- “Smooth operator” as an optional playful status phrase, not a permanent slogan.
- “Still we rise” / “Never give up” style motivational wording should be original and not presented as a driver quote unless verified and licensed for use.
- A “Veteran Mode” or “Racecraft” preset can be an original theme name.
- Do not use driver photos, signatures, helmet designs, numbers as identity marks, or team logos without permission.

## Accessibility and comfort
- WCAG-informed contrast for text and controls.
- Minimum 48dp touch targets where practical.
- Clock supports large text and high contrast.
- Blur and motion are optional.
- Do not encode state using color alone.
- Test with TalkBack, display size scaling and color correction.
