# 01 — Product Vision

## Vision
Create an Android launcher that feels calm at rest and capable in motion: a natural wallpaper and clock at the centre, with productivity and app access one gesture away.

## Positioning
A minimalist, gesture-first launcher with racing-inspired confidence and practical organization. It borrows useful patterns from productivity launchers—spaces, app groups, search, widgets and focus controls—while maintaining an original visual identity.

## Target users
- People who prefer a clean home screen.
- Students and professionals who want focused workspaces.
- Android users who enjoy customization and gesture navigation.
- F1 fans who appreciate subtle racing references, not a full racing-game interface.

## Core product principles
1. **Quiet by default:** no icon grid, dock, or persistent search bar on the default home screen.
2. **Fast by design:** common actions should take one gesture and one tap.
3. **Predictable gestures:** gestures must be discoverable, configurable and conflict-aware.
4. **Native Android respect:** use platform APIs and preserve expected system behaviour.
5. **Material You foundation:** dynamic color where available, with a stable fallback palette.
6. **Personality in the details:** cheeky copy is optional and never blocks a task.
7. **Privacy by default:** collect no analytics or personal content unless explicitly introduced and disclosed.
8. **Accessible by default:** readable contrast, scalable text, touch targets and reduced-motion support.

## Differentiators
- Wallpaper-first clock home screen.
- Two distinct widget destinations: launcher productivity widgets and Android app widgets.
- Spaces for separate contexts such as Study, Personal and Work.
- Racing-inspired microcopy and optional themes.
- A quick radial/favourite-app interaction that remains secondary to the clean home screen.

## Non-goals for v1
- Replacing the Android lock screen.
- Replacing the notification shade or Quick Settings.
- Building a full digital wellbeing service.
- Building a custom music streaming service.
- Cloud sync or accounts before local functionality is stable.
- Copying Phi Launcher’s exact layouts, names, artwork or implementation.
- Using official Formula 1, FIA, team or driver assets without licences.

## Success measures
- Launcher can be selected as default Home app.
- App drawer opens and launches apps reliably.
- Home screen remains visually quiet.
- Gestures work consistently across supported screen sizes.
- Cold start and home-return performance meet the budgets in `10_PERFORMANCE_RELIABILITY.md`.
- No data loss during normal process death or app update.
- Users can understand the navigation without a tutorial.
